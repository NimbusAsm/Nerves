# Security policy

Nerves is an identity product: a vulnerability here affects every deployment.

## Reporting

**Do not open a public issue.** Email the maintainers with:

- affected component and version/commit,
- reproduction steps,
- impact assessment if known.

We aim to acknowledge within 72 hours and to ship a fix within 90 days, coordinated
with the reporter. Credit is given unless you ask otherwise.

## Scope

In scope: authentication and session handling, token issuance and validation,
authorization decisions, tenant/organization isolation, audit integrity,
credential storage, and supply-chain integrity of published artifacts.

Out of scope: findings that require a compromised host or a malicious administrator.
